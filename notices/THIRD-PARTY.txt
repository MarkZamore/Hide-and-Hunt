Hide and Hunt for LANMinecraft

Hide and Hunt by ItzMaceTekk is MIT-licensed. The exact author release is
Modrinth FaWrAGlY (1.0.0-beta.2+26.1-26.3).
https://modrinth.com/plugin/hide-and-hunt/version/FaWrAGlY

The original bundle includes e4mc 6.2.3+modern by Stara Gulli (Skye), MIT.
This downstream distribution resolves ban-command hook conflicts, removes
e4mc's owner-login bypass, and disables the experimental Dialtone resolver
and login hooks. The ordinary generated-address relay stays available.
https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/e4mc
https://github.com/vgskye/e4mc-minecraft-architectury

https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fabric-api
https://github.com/FabricMC/fabric

e4mc contains Kaleido Config 0.3.3+1.3.2, LGPL-3.0, with relocated Quilt
Config 1.3.2 (Apache-2.0) and Night Config 3.8.1 (LGPL-3.0) components.
The corresponding source and upstream build instructions are available at:
https://github.com/sleepingdragoninn/kaleido-config/tree/0bc38fea0b586bae20f2ce296851592498bfdc0a
